Hormone-Friendly Food Timing Guide

1. Suggested Meals by Time of Day

Morning (7–10 AM)
Slow + moderate carbs, protein, healthy fats, digestion-supportive drinks.
Best exercise window: 30–60 minutes after a light breakfast.

  • Greek yogurt + almonds + berries + kefir
  • Steel-cut oats + chia seeds + blueberries + whole milk
  • Whole-grain toast + avocado + ricotta + eggs
  • Quinoa bowl + walnuts + apple + yogurt
  • Smoothie with whole milk

Midday (11 AM–2 PM)
Moderate carbs, balanced meals, strongest digestion window.

  • Turkey wrap + avocado + cheddar
  • Salmon bowl + quinoa + broccoli + feta
  • Lentil salad + tomatoes + feta
  • Chicken + barley + vegetables

Afternoon (3–5 PM)
Minimal carbs, protein-based snacks.

  • Hummus + vegetables
  • Cottage cheese + tomatoes
  • Greek yogurt + walnuts

Evening (6–8 PM)
Low-carb meals, vegetables, protein.

  • Salmon + asparagus
  • Chicken + Brussels sprouts + sauerkraut
  • Tempeh + kale + cauliflower rice

Bedtime
Protein-only snacks.

  • Cottage cheese + almonds
  • Greek yogurt + flaxseed
  • Kefir + cinnamon

2. Shopping List Organized by Carb Type

Slow Carbs

High-fiber, complex-starch, low-sugar carbs. Best time: Morning + Midday.

  • Steel-cut oats
  • Lentils
  • Chickpeas
  • Black beans
  • Berries
  • Apples
  • Pears

Moderate Carbs

Medium-speed digesting carbs. Best time: Midday.

  • Quinoa
  • Brown rice
  • Farro
  • Barley
  • Whole-grain toast
  • Sweet potato

Minimal Carbs

Very small carb amounts. Best time: Afternoon.

  • Carrots
  • Cucumbers
  • Bell peppers
  • Celery
  • Nuts
  • Hummus

Low Carbs

Low-starch, high-fiber vegetables. Best time: Evening.

  • Broccoli
  • Cauliflower
  • Brussels sprouts
  • Kale
  • Zucchini
  • Sauerkraut

Protein Sources

  • Greek yogurt (full-fat)
  • Cottage cheese (full-fat)
  • Kefir (full-fat)
  • Whole milk
  • Cheddar, Swiss, gouda, mozzarella
  • Eggs, turkey, chicken, salmon, beef

3. Hormone Tutorial

Insulin

Responds strongly to carbs. Controlled by slow carbs + protein + fat.

Cortisol

Peaks in the morning, declines at night.

Estrogen

Supported by cruciferous vegetables and fermented foods.

Testosterone

Supported by protein, omega-3 fats, zinc, magnesium.

Digestion & Glucose Absorption Timeline

Stomach processing: 2–5 hours
Glucose absorption begins: 15–30 minutes after entering the small intestine
Full absorption window: 3–5 hours
Total stomach + intestine time: ~6 hours
